ENQUIRY PRIVACY

Your enquiry, handled with care.

This explains what happens when you request a call or contact the academy.

What the callback form collects

Your name, adult contact number, program interest, learning preference, preferred call window and your agreement to be contacted. It also records the page and campaign label that brought you here, so the academy can understand which shared links bring enquiries.

How it is used

The academy uses your details to respond to your request and arrange a conversation or introductory session. Callback requests are stored in the academy’s Netlify Forms dashboard and are not published on this website. Please do not submit children’s personal details.

Calls and WhatsApp

Call links open your device’s dialler. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, where you review and send your own message. A click is not proof that a phone call took place.

Contact or removal requests

To correct your enquiry, withdraw your contact request or ask for removal, contact the teacher on +919866219903 or glakshmiabacus@gmail.com. The site uses session storage for campaign labels when available; it does not add advertising trackers.

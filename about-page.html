ABOUT THE ACADEMY

A little curiosity can go a long way.

At The Numbers Academy, we want children to feel comfortable exploring numbers, asking questions and taking on new challenges.

Learning that starts
with understanding.

Our academy in Vidya Nagar, Hyderabad offers abacus training, Vedic Maths and Rubik’s Cube programs. Online abacus options help families explore learning from home.

We bring together practical tools and structured exercises, with a focus on building arithmetic foundations through practice.
